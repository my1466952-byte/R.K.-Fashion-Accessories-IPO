# R.K. Fashion Accessories IPO: Key Details and What to Watch

The **R.K. Fashion Accessories IPO** is attracting attention as the company enters the SME IPO market with a fresh issue of around ₹35 crore. The company is involved in manufacturing through contract manufacturers and wholesale distribution of imitation jewellery, along with the trading of branded cosmetics.

## R.K. Fashion Accessories IPO – Key Details

- **IPO Open Date:** October 5, 2026
- **IPO Close Date:** October 7, 2026
- **Price Band:** ₹77 – ₹82 per share
- **Issue Size:** Around ₹35 crore
- **Total Shares:** Approximately 42.67 lakh shares
- **Expected Listing Date:** October 12, 2026
- **IPO Type:** SME IPO

## Business Overview

R.K. Fashion Accessories is expanding across both **B2B and B2C markets**. Its business strategy includes wholesale distribution, direct retail sales and online channels.

The company is also developing its retail presence in Kolkata, which could help increase its reach among individual customers and strengthen its B2C business.

## Financial Performance

For FY2025-26, the company reported:

| Financial Year | Total Income | Profit After Tax |
|---|---:|---:|
| FY2025-26 | ₹31.54 Cr | ₹6.29 Cr |
| FY2024-25 | ₹17.82 Cr | ₹2.00 Cr |

The financial performance shows significant improvement in both income and profitability.

## Use of IPO Proceeds

The IPO proceeds are planned to be used for several business requirements, including:

- Working capital
- Setting up a new plating facility
- Proposed B2B showroom
- B2C stores in Kolkata
- General corporate purposes

## Key Risks to Watch

Investors should also consider some important risks associated with the company:

- Dependence on contract manufacturers
- Regional concentration of business
- Working-capital requirements
- Competition in the jewellery and cosmetics segments
